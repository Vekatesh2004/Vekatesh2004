## About Me

Hi there! I'm Venkatesh, an MSc Bioinformatics student at REVA University, Bengaluru, interested in computational biology, genomics, antimicrobial resistance research, and data-driven scientific discovery.

I work across bioinformatics and software development, using Python, R, Linux, and reproducible workflows to analyze biological data. My work includes microbial whole-genome sequencing, antimicrobial-resistance gene analysis, transcriptomics, phylogenetics, and AI-enabled healthcare and fintech applications.

### 1. Antimicrobial Resistance Analysis in *Acinetobacter baumannii*

**Domain:** Bioinformatics | Microbial Genomics | Antimicrobial Resistance

A computational research workflow focused on identifying antimicrobial-resistance genes and analyzing bacterial whole-genome sequencing data, including research context associated with *Acinetobacter baumannii* and accession ERP130079.

- Analyze paired-end WGS sequencing data.
- Perform read quality control and genome assembly.
- Annotate assemblies and assess assembly quality.
- Identify resistance genes using CARD/RGI and related AMR resources.
- Summarize results for downstream comparison and visualization.

**Technologies:** fastp, FastQC, MultiQC, SPAdes, QUAST, BUSCO, Prokka, RGI/CARD, Python, R, Linux.
